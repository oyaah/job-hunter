# Reflect

Compress raw learnings into a few durable principles so the system gets sharper without growing noisier. This is the self-evolving step — and it's cheap, because *you* (the model) do the distilling by editing files; no tool, no extra call.

## When
When `learnings.md` has accumulated enough new lines since the last reflect (a dozen or so), or when the user asks. The hunt loop suggests it at natural breaks.

## What to do
1. Read `learnings.md` and the current `voice-profile.md` / `targeting-prefs.md`.
2. Distill the new learnings into **3–5 durable principles** — what to do, what to avoid, contradictions resolved. **Patch** the profile files (keep what still holds, change what the new learnings overturned); don't blank-slate rewrite. Stay tight — these are loaded before every draft, so keep them compact (curate, don't pile on).
3. Mark in `learnings.md` how far you've distilled (e.g. a `<!-- distilled up to 2026-06-06 -->` marker) so you don't re-process the same lines. Keep the raw learnings as the audit trail.

## Principles
- Patch, don't rewrite from scratch. Preserve working content.
- Curate, don't accumulate — the profiles stay small on purpose.
- Effects apply next time the profiles are loaded, never mid-draft.
